About Me

Music has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. As a child, I was constantly listening to music and drawing inspiration from a wide range of genres, especially Alternative Rock, as well as Soul, R&B, and Funk. In my early teens, I picked up the guitar, which quickly became my greatest passion and creative outlet. Since then, I have learned trumpet, keyboards, and bass, and have been playing regularly in bands across many different genres. I love expressing myself through songwriting, producing, and singing backing vocals, and creating music continues to be one of the most important parts of who I am.
