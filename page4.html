Studio work has become one of my greatest creative passions. I regularly contribute to productions as a guitarist and instrumentalist, while also taking part in songwriting sessions, developing songs from the ground up, and producing music that brings each artist's unique vision to life. My own solo album, released under my birth name Paul Hain, was entirely written, produced, recorded, and released by myself between 2022 and 2023, when I was just beginning to explore music production during the COVID period. Although my production skills have grown significantly since then, that project remains an honest reflection of the curiosity and creativity that started it all. Whether working on my own music or collaborating with others, I strive to create productions that feel authentic, emotional, and timeless.
