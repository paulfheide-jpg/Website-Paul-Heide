My Artist Projects:

  • MUNDAY – Best Music for the Worst Day of the Week.                                                                            Soul Pop with heart, groove, and character. Combining old-school Motown influences with modern catchy hooks, MUNDAY blends soulful ballads, infectious energy, handcrafted beats, and jazzy vibes into a sound that feels timeless yet fresh. Music made to lift your mood, move your feet, and stay on your mind.                                                                                                        
  • "Garage Tracks"                                                                                                                               Released under my birth name, Paul Heide, this solo album captures an important chapter of my musical journey. I wrote the songs during my final years of school and student life, and independently produced, recorded, and released the entire project myself. The album reflects the musical influences that shaped me at the time while documenting my growth as a songwriter, musician, and producer. Although my skills in production, songwriting, and performance have evolved significantly since then, these recordings capture a period of exploration and discovery. It is precisely this imperfection that gives the album its raw, honest, and authentic character—a snapshot of the years when I began taking music seriously and found my true passion.
